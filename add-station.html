Run an FM, AM or online radio station in the Philippines? FilRadio lists it for free, with its own pin on the map, so listeners at home and Filipinos abroad can play it in one tap. Fill in the form below and send it to us by email. We usually add new stations within a few days.

What we need from you

  1. Station name and frequency, for example "Radyo Bandera 97.3" or "DZRH 666". The frequency decides whether you show under FM or AM. Online-only stations need just the name.
  2. A direct stream link that starts with https://, such as an Icecast or Shoutcast address, or a link ending in .mp3, .aac or .m3u8. Facebook Live, YouTube and web pages with a player on them won't work. Ask your streaming provider for the "direct stream URL" if you're not sure. Links with only an IP address and http:// can't play on secure sites.
  3. City and province, so your station gets a pin in the right place on the map.
  4. Logo: a square image, at least 200 by 200 pixels, as a link (from your website or Facebook page) or attached to the email.
  5. Website or Facebook page, and a few words on what you play (OPM, news, Bisaya, Christian, etc.).

Use the Test stream button below to check your stream link plays here before you send it.

Send your station

"Send by email" opens your email app with everything filled in, addressed to zendzdev@gmail.com. No email app? Tap "Copy details" and paste them into an email from any device.

Good to know

  • Listing is free. Your stream plays straight from your own server; FilRadio never re-hosts or records it.
  • We check every stream regularly. Stations that stay off air for a long time are marked off air.
  • Need a change or want your station removed? Email us and we'll update it.
