Last updated: 15 November 2026

FilRadio (filradio.xyz) is a free website for listening to Philippine radio stations on a map. You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. This page explains what data is collected when you use the site, and by whom.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

FilRadio shows ads from Google AdSense to help pay for the site. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this website and to other websites.

  • Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners show you ads based on your visits to FilRadio and other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of other vendors' cookies for personalised ads at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (through Google Tag Manager) to see how many people visit and which features they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, your device and browser type, and your approximate location, which is worked out from your IP address. We use this only as overall statistics to improve FilRadio. See Google's Privacy Policy for more.

FilRadio also keeps a simple visit counter that adds +1 to a shared total, at most once per device per day, along with the type of site you came from (for example "Facebook" or "Google search"). The counter runs on our own server at Cloudflare and holds no personal information.

Your location

If you tap the "near me" button, your browser asks for permission to share your location. It is used only on your device to find nearby stations. It is never sent to us or stored.

Data stored on your device

FilRadio saves your favourite stations, theme, map style and a copy of the station list in your browser's local storage, so the site is fast and remembers your settings. This data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Listener chat

The chat only connects when you open it. Messages and the display name you choose are public to everyone in the chat and are deleted automatically after 6 hours. To stop spam and abuse, the chat server (run on Cloudflare) keeps a one-way scrambled code made from your IP address, never the address itself, to apply rate limits, warnings and mutes. These records are deleted 30 days after the last warning. Your chosen name is saved in your browser so you don't have to type it again. By default your messages show the flag of the country you are connecting from (as reported by Cloudflare, country only, never your city or address); you can turn this off under "Change name". Messages with swearing, sexual content, links or contact details are blocked automatically.

Radio streams and map tiles

When you play a station, your browser connects directly to that station's streaming server. Map images load from OpenFreeMap, EOX and Amazon Web Services. Like any website, these services can see your IP address and browser details. Their own privacy policies apply.

Children

FilRadio is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
